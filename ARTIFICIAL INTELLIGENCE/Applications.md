### Following are some applications of different types of AI.

## Machine Learning
1. Email spam filter: Learns from past emails to mark new emails as spam or not spam.

2. Weather prediction app: Learns from past weather data to predict rain or temperature.

## Deep Learning

1. Protecting Privacy in Training Materials : AI generates realistic human faces that are not real people.
 
2. Used in safety systems, simulations, industrial training: Some events happen very rarely: accidents , equipment failures, unusual conditions.
 AI generates synthetic examples of these rare cases so models can still learn.

## Generative AI
1. AI image generators: Creates new images from text like “a cat riding a bicycle”.

2. AI tools writing emails or essays: Generates fresh content instead of just copying.

## Natural Language Processing
1. Chatbots in customer support: Reads your message and replies in human language

2. Voice Typing on Mobile: Converts spoken words into written text while you speak.

## Computer Vision
1. Traffic signal cameras : Detect vehicles and control traffic lights. 

2. Medical X‑ray analysis : Helps doctors detect problems from images.
